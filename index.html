Continuous corner · G1 vs G2

Squircle Workbench

Apple's continuous corner is not a superellipse. It is three pieces spliced together: cubic Bézier → arc → cubic Bézier. The whole construction falls out of the radius r and a smoothing factor s: the corner consumes p = (1+s)·r of each edge, keeps a 90°(1−s) arc in the middle, and lets a Bézier on either side ramp the curvature up from zero. iOS measures p = 1.528665 r, i.e. s ≈ 0.5287.
